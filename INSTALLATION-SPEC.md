# Villa Raava — Installation and RFQ Specification

Revision: 2026-10-19

## Contract authority

1. The numbered station map and procurement schedule in the published plan control species, quantity, spacing and location.
2. Design visualizations are illustrative only. They do not authorize a species, quantity, flower colour, supplied size or physical position.
3. Existing plants identified as retained are excluded from the purchase schedule.
4. No substitution, purchase, installation or irreversible work proceeds without Max's written approval.

## Nursery RFQ return requirements

For every line, return:

- accepted botanical name and Indonesian/trade name;
- current available quantity;
- supplied plant height and spread;
- pot/polybag diameter and volume;
- unit price and validity period;
- current batch photograph with a scale reference;
- expected lead time;
- proposed substitution, if any, on a separate line.

A proposed substitute must match the specified light, water regime, mature height/spread, growth habit, maintenance burden and design role. It is not accepted until approved in writing.

## Pre-install hold point

Before award, the contractor must field-verify every zone hold point shown in the plan. At minimum:

- Zone 1: soil versus concrete and the full heater/service approach;
- Zone 2: glass-door step-in and drainage;
- Zone 3: all equipment access pockets and the upstairs sightline;
- Zone 4: root collars, net infill area and pool-edge containment;
- Zone 5: reuse condition of existing plants and hanging-point load capacity;
- Zone 6: heliconia regrowth, 1.1–1.2 m clear route and 2.2 m head clearance;
- Zone 7: gate swing, sign, motorcycle envelope and bougainvillea-pot drainage;
- Zone 8: clear planter length, width, soil depth, saturated load and drainage;
- Zone 9: corner-pot internal size, shower-planter dimensions and drainage.

Any changed quantity must be recalculated from the stated spacing formula and returned as a marked-up schedule before approval.

## Stock acceptance

Reject plants that are incorrectly labelled, root-bound, unstable, badly scorched, pest-affected, mechanically damaged or materially smaller than the approved supplied grade. Confirm roots are moist but not waterlogged. Keep palms' root balls and growing points intact.

## Set-out and planting

1. Mark all no-plant/access zones first.
2. Dry-set every plant at its numbered station before holes are dug or pots filled.
3. Confirm sightlines, mature clearance and access with Max or his representative.
4. Keep palm root collars visible; do not mound soil against trunks.
5. Keep foliage, soil and mulch out of pools, drains, doors, paths, glass-cleaning zones, AC exhaust and service equipment.
6. Use free-draining planting media appropriate to each container/bed. Soil, compost, mulch, irrigation and fertilizer are outside the plant-price allowance and require a separate quote.
7. Establish a separate dry irrigation regime for bougainvillea. Roofed zones require reliable hand watering or drip; wet-season open beds must not remain on fixed irrigation when soil is wet.

## Handover evidence

The contractor provides:

- one photo of each station immediately after set-out and after planting;
- final delivered quantities by botanical name;
- batch/receipt references and any approved substitutions;
- irrigation test evidence;
- a 30-day establishment visit and defect/replacement list;
- pruning limits: Zone 6 canopy above 2.2 m, Zone 8 Murraya at 40–60 cm, and facade trails at the approved maintained length.

## Commercial exclusions

The schedule's budget is plant-only. It excludes soil, compost, mulch, planters, irrigation, drainage, fertilizer, labour, delivery, access equipment, bin enclosure, lighting, structural/hanging hardware and waste removal unless separately quoted and approved.
